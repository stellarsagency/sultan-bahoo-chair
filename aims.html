Our Mission

Aims & Objectives

Committed to preserving and promoting the spiritual and intellectual heritage of Sultan Bahoo (RA)

Preserve Manuscripts

To collect and preserve manuscripts of Abyat and other works of Sultan Bahoo (RA).

Research Library

Establish Sultan Bahoo research library for scholars and researchers.

Audio Visual Archive

Establish audio visual catalog and collection of singers who sang poetry of Sultan Bahoo.

Sarwari Qadri Heritage

Collect and preserve material about Sufis of Sarwari Qadri order.

Research Journal

Publish a biannual research journal on study of Sultan Bahoo and Sufism.

Publish Sufi Poetry

Publish and promote Sufi poetry and philosophy of Sultan Bahoo.

Supervise Research

Supervise and assist research scholars of M.Phil and Ph.D.

Conferences & Seminars

Hold conferences, seminars, symposia, lectures and talks about Sultan Bahoo.

Academic Collaboration

Link and co-operate with similar chairs and research centers working in other universities of Pakistan and abroad.

Jashan-e-Bahoo

Hold an annual Jashan-e-Bahoo to celebrate the spiritual legacy of Sultan Bahoo.

Extension Lectures

Arrange extension lectures on Sufism and Sultan Bahoo in rural and urban areas to neutralize extremism prevalent in Pakistani society.

Sultan Bahoo Chair

Dedicated to the preservation and promotion of the spiritual teachings and scholarly legacy of Sultan Bahoo (RA).
