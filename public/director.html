Message of Sultan Bahoo is universal message of love, peace, humanity and interfaith harmony. It's high time to bring these teachings in academic circles and enlighten our ranks with potent content of Sufi philosophy.


Our all efforts to curb extremism and terrorism are useless until not blended with Sufi teachings. Sultan Bahoo is the most vocal Sufi Saint and Philosopher against all these evils but unfortunately he has been ignored in academic and research circles for many reasons.


With the establishment of Sultan Bahoo Chair in University of Art and Culture Jamshoro, a new chapter of research and propagation of teachings of Sultan Bahoo will open by adopting all new modes and methods of academia for this purpose.

Dr. Saghar Abro

Director
